Three grades. No guesswork.

Total transparency: our certification levels make the condition, history and value of every vehicle unmistakable.

Platinum

"The Collector's Standard."
Body condition100% original factory parts. Zero body panel replacements — not even a bumper.
Mechanical statusFlawless. Passed all advanced engine and transmission diagnostics.

Gold
