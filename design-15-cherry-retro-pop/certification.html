SD Certified Standards

Three grades. No guesswork.

Total transparency: our certification levels make the condition, history and value of every vehicle unmistakable.

★

Platinum

"The Collector's Standard."
  • Body condition — 100% original factory parts. Zero body panel replacements, not even a bumper.
  • Mechanical status — Flawless. Passed all advanced engine and transmission diagnostics.
  • Warranty — Maximum protection: OEM or Premium Third-Party Extended Warranty (Engine & Transmission).
★

Gold

"Smart Premium."
  • Body condition — High integrity. Original chassis; minor bolt-on parts (bumper, hood, door) may have been replaced.
  • Mechanical status — Fully fit and top-tier verified — drives exactly like a Platinum car, but has lived a real life on the road.
  • Warranty — Fully protected: OEM or eligible for Premium Third-Party Extended Warranty (Engine & Transmission).
★

Silver

"True Value."
  • Body condition — Structurally safe. No major accidents. Minor cosmetic wear consistent with age.
  • Mechanical status — Road-worthy. Runs well but may require minor maintenance in the near future.
  • Warranty — Economy deal: not extended-eligible; savings passed directly to the buyer through lower pricing.
