The Red Series · No. 01Buyer's Edition

The used car,
re-examined.

Every car in our marketplace carries a 300-point inspection report, an AI-calculated score and a guaranteed grade — inspected in person by Suresh Drives technicians, photographed flaw by flaw. No broker. No guesswork.

SD Certified is the marketplace arm of Suresh Drives — India's first professional on-site car inspection company, based in Kozhikode. What began as pre-purchase checks for nervous buyers became a full certified marketplace: 1,248 cars live, each with its report published before the deal.

This edition walks through how the marketplace works — from the first search to the final handover — and shows you exactly what "certified" means on paper. Turn the page and look at a car sheet the way our technicians do.
