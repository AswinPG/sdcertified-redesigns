SD Certified Standards

Three grades. No guesswork.

Total transparency: our certification levels make the condition, history and value of every vehicle unmistakable.

Platinum

"The Collector's Standard."
  • Body condition100% original factory parts. Zero body panel replacements — not even a bumper.
  • Mechanical statusFlawless. Passed all advanced engine and transmission diagnostics.
  • WarrantyMaximum protection — OEM warranty or Premium Third-Party Extended Warranty (Engine & Transmission).

Gold

"Smart Premium."
  • Body conditionHigh integrity. Original chassis, but minor bolt-on parts (bumper, hood, door) may have been replaced.
  • Mechanical statusFully fit. Engine and transmission health verified as top-tier — drives exactly like a Platinum car.
  • WarrantyFully protected — OEM warranty or eligible for Premium Third-Party Extended Warranty (Engine & Transmission).

Silver

"True Value."
  • Body conditionStructurally safe. No major accidents. Minor cosmetic wear consistent with age.
  • Mechanical statusRoad-worthy. Runs well but may require minor maintenance in the near future.
  • WarrantyEconomy deal — not eligible for extended warranty due to age or model; savings passed directly to the buyer through lower pricing.
