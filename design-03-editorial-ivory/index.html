An Offering of Suresh Drives

Suresh Drives is India's first professional on-site car inspection company. For over a decade, from its headquarters in Kozhikode, Kerala, it has introduced advanced tools and AI-powered report calculations to the used-car trade — inspecting for buyers and sellers alike, with independence as the only loyalty.

SD Certified is that expertise, productised: every car on this marketplace has been touched by those same technicians, photographed panel by panel, and graded Platinum, Gold or Silver. The 300+ point process detects what the eye misses — structural damage, flood history, tampered odometers.

The Method

Three steps to certainty.

01

Book an inspection

Pick a date, time and location — at your home or the seller's. We handle used-car pre-purchase checks and new-car PDI before delivery.

02

300-point check, AI-scored report

Structure and accident history, engine bay and diagnostics, body, paint depth and glass, tyres and brakes, electricals and interior — then a full road test. Every finding photographed.

03

List or buy with confidence

The car is graded and goes live with its report attached. Found a flaw, or could not test something? You will see it in the report, not hear about it later.
