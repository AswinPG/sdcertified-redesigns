The car you can actually know.

There is a version of buying a used car where you never have to wonder. It starts with a technician standing in front of the engine bay, counting to three hundred. This is that version.

SD Certified is the marketplace arm of Suresh Drives — India's first professional on-site car inspection company, based in Kozhikode, Kerala. For over a decade the team has inspected cars for buyers and sellers across India; now every car that passes their 300-point check goes live here, with its report attached before any deal is discussed.

"If a flaw exists, it goes in the report. The buyer should see it before the deal, not after."

The grades are simple — Platinum, Gold, Silver — and they mean the same thing on every car, because the same people check them all. Browse the lineup when you're ready; the reports are public.
