The process behind the grade.

Eight areas, three hundred points, one rule: show every flaw. Follow what happens between "car arrives" and "grade published".

1Structure & accident historyChassis scanned for bends, kinks and repair marks. Flood markers checked at every drain point.
2Engine bay & diagnosticsDTC scan, fluid checks, leak inspection under load. Zero active faults to pass.
3Body & paint depthGauge readings on every panel. Resprays detected and photographed — no exceptions.
4Glass & visibilityCracks, chips and wiper condition on all glass. Driver line-of-sight checked first.
5Tyres & brakesTread depth, uneven wear and brake pad thickness measured, not eyeballed.
6Electricals & interiorEvery switch, sensor and seat mechanism tested. Infotainment and ADAS verified.
7Full road testSuspension, steering and drivetrain under real load — highway and city segments.
✓AI-scored report & gradeEvery finding photographed and scored. The grade — Platinum, Gold or Silver — is published with the listing.
