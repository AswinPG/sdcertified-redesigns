What exactly is included in the ₹9,999 Standard package?

Everything needed to list and sell: a 200+ point pre-sales inspection by Suresh Drives technicians, a professional photo shoot, an inspection & presentation video, Meta ads promotion support — and you keep custody of your vehicle throughout.

Does SD Certified charge brokerage?

No. We are not a broker. You deal directly with the buyer and we never take a percentage of the sale price. Our packages are fixed and published here.

What happens after my car is inspected?

Your car receives an SD Certified grade — Platinum, Gold or Silver — based on our 300-point process. The full photo-backed report is attached to your listing so buyers know exactly what they're getting.

I live outside India. Can I sell through SD Certified?

Yes. Many of our sellers and buyers are NRI owners. We handle communication, verification and paperwork end-to-end — several clients in Dubai and the Gulf rely on the team for exactly this.

Can I get my car inspected before deciding to list?

Absolutely. You can book a standalone pre-purchase or pre-sale inspection through Suresh Drives at your location, review the AI-powered report, and then decide whether to list on SD Certified.
