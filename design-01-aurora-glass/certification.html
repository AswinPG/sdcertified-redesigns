SD Certified Standards.

Total transparency: our certification levels make the condition, history and value of every vehicle unmistakable. Three grades — no guesswork.

Platinum

"The Collector's Standard."
  • Body condition: 100% original factory parts. Zero body panel replacements — not even a bumper.
  • Mechanical status: Flawless. Passed all advanced engine and transmission diagnostics.
  • Warranty: Maximum protection — OEM warranty or Premium Third-Party Extended Warranty (Engine & Transmission).

Gold

"Smart Premium."
  • Body condition: High integrity. Original chassis, but minor bolt-on parts (bumper, hood, door) may have been replaced.
  • Mechanical status: Fully fit — engine and transmission health verified as top-tier. Drives exactly like a Platinum car.
  • Warranty: Fully protected — OEM warranty or eligible for Premium Third-Party Extended Warranty (Engine & Transmission).

Silver

"True Value."
  • Body condition: Structurally safe. No major accidents. Minor cosmetic wear consistent with age.
  • Mechanical status: Road-worthy. Runs well but may require minor maintenance in the near future.
  • Warranty: Economy deal — not extended-eligible due to age or model; savings passed directly to the buyer through lower pricing.
What technicians check

The 300-point process, in eight lines.

Structure & accident historyChassis scanned for bends, kinks and repair marks.
Engine bay & diagnosticsDTC scan, fluid checks, leak inspection under load.
Body & paint depthGauge readings on every panel, respray detection.
Glass & visibilityCracks, chips and wiper condition on all glass.
Tyres & brakesTread depth, uneven wear and brake pad thickness.
Electricals & interiorEvery switch, sensor and seat mechanism tested.
Full road testSuspension, steering and drivetrain under real load.
AI-scored reportEvery finding photographed and scored — no omissions.
